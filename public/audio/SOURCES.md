# Audio source notes

`jeopardy-main-classic.mp3` is the 1984-1991 Jeopardy main theme recording from the Internet Archive item [Jeopardy - 1984-1991 - Theme Song](https://archive.org/details/tvtunes_29603), and `jeopardy-main-classic.ogg` is a format conversion of that MP3. It is the main theme rather than the "Think Music" cue, but is not the exact Geek Music recording linked by the event organizer at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=afz8WmqAUM4. It is intended for the private SLxCA event; the repo and its Cloudflare assets may be publicly accessible. Replace the asset before any use that requires rights beyond this event.

`showtime-theme`, `thinking-pulse`, `buzz-in`, and `answer-reveal` are original synthesized compositions created for the game. They are used only by the Sighton/default theme.
